Privacy

Adage collects no data. It works entirely offline — no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no network requests of any kind.

The only data stored is the index of the quote currently displayed by each widget instance and the timestamp of the last daily advance — both kept locally on your device in Android's standard app-private storage. When you remove a widget, its data is deleted. When you uninstall the app, all data is removed by Android.
